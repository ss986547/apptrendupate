## 1. User Pain Patterns This Week

1.  **"My photos and videos are a mess, and my phone is always full."** Users struggle with digital clutter, especially in their photo libraries, leading to storage issues and difficulty finding specific memories.
    *   **Sources:** AppStore-Utilities #4 "Cleanup: Phone Storage Cleaner" indicates a high demand for solutions in this area.
    *   **Why current solutions fail:** Many existing "cleaner" apps are either too generic, too aggressive, or have intrusive monetization, lacking intelligent, privacy-preserving suggestions for what to keep vs. delete. Manual cleanup is tedious.

2.  **"I want to improve my creative skills, but I need real-time, intelligent feedback, not just post-editing."** Aspiring creators (photographers, videographers) desire immediate guidance to enhance their output during the creation process.
    *   **Sources:** Product Hunt: "AureaCam: Real-time scoring to master the rule of thirds" directly addresses this.
    *   **Why current solutions fail:** Standard camera apps offer basic features or filters, while professional apps focus on manual controls. Few provide live, AI-driven compositional feedback *before* the shot is taken, leaving users to learn through trial-and-error or post-processing.

3.  **"I need to separate audio components (vocals, instruments) from music, but I want it local and private."** Musicians, DJs, and content creators require tools to isolate parts of a song for various purposes, but are wary of cloud-based solutions or find desktop tools too complex.
    *   **Sources:** Hacker News: "StemDeck, a free, open-source and local AI stem separator" (52 pts). Product Hunt: "StemDeck" also listed.
    *   **Why current solutions fail:** Many powerful stem separation tools are cloud-based, raising privacy concerns and requiring internet access. Desktop solutions are often expensive, complex, or not mobile-first.
