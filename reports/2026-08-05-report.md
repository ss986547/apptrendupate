## 1. User Pain Patterns This Week

1.  **"I'm stuck in an endless loop trying to authenticate to a security app on the same device, especially after getting a new phone."**
    *   **Sources:** This is the most prominent pain, appearing in dozens of Microsoft Authenticator 1-star reviews (e.g., "Authenticate your log in to Authenticator with the code in Authenticator", "How am I supposed to scan QR code when the QR code is on my phone browser", "Stuck on a screen showing me the number", "Looping", "Worst app ever").
    *   **Why current solutions fail:** Microsoft Authenticator has a fundamental UX flaw in its setup and recovery flow, particularly for single-device users or those switching devices. It often assumes a second device for authentication or a perfectly smooth backup, which frequently isn't the case, leading to account lockouts.

2.  **"My critical authentication data is lost or inaccessible when I switch phones or if the backup fails."**
    *   **Sources:** Microsoft Authenticator 1-star reviews ("В один прекрасный день потерялись все данные", "Lost my entire account forever because I didn’t know I had to backup my account", "Corrupted back up file", "Need to reset the account each time I change a device").
    *   **Why current solutions fail:** Existing authentication apps have inadequate, unreliable, or poorly communicated backup/restore mechanisms
